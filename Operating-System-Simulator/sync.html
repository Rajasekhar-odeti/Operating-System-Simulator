Mutex & Semaphores

A semaphore acts as a system signaling mechanism. It maintains an internal permit counter that dictates how many resources (or critical locks) are available globally.

Threads invoke acquire() to enter the critical section. If no permits remain, they are suspended in a wait queue until a permit is freed via release().
